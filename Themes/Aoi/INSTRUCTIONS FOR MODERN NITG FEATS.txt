*Stitch.lua:
    -Copy Lua, Screens folders and config.lua file over
    -In Graphics folder, copy OverlayScreen footer.redir and OverlayScreen header.redir over
    -In Scripts folder, copy Stitch.lua over
    -In Sounds folder, copy gta-wasted.ogg and srt_hit.ogg over, which are the sounds for the two default FailOverlays.
    -In metrics.ini, at [Common], change it to this:
    [Common]
    ...
    InitialScreen=InitializeScripts
    OverlayScreens=ScreenSystemLayer,ScreenSyncOverlay,ScreenDebugOverlay,OverlayScreen

    [InitializeScripts]
    Class=ScreenStage
    NextScreen= !!!(whatever the original InitialScreen= is)!!!
    InitCommand=%stx("lua.event").OverlayReady
    MinimumDelay=0

    [OverlayScreen]
    Class=ScreenTestInput
    InputsOnCommand=luaeffect,Update;zoom,0.7;horizalign,left;diffusealpha,0;
    InputsUpdateCommand=%function(a) stx("lua.keyboard").KeyHandler(a) end
    TimerOnCommand=hidden,1
    DevicesOnCommand=hidden,1
    
    In BGAnimations folder, somewhere in ScreenGameplay (here it's in ScreenGameplay underlay), put:
    <ActorFrame
		OnCommand="%function(self)
			self:stopeffect()
			if FUCK_EXE then
				stitch('lua.death').Start()
			end
		end" 
	/>
    
*Offset Plot: (This is done entirely in the BGAnimations folder)
    -Copy OffsetPlot.xml over.
    -Put the corresponding offset plot codes in their corresponding screens. In Aoi they're at ScreenStage overlay, ScreenGameplay overlay,
    and ScreenEvaluation overlay. It doesn't have to be 'overlay', it just needs to be somewhere in the correct screen,
    'underlay', 'in', 'background', etc. is fine.

*basic_nitg_v491_feats.lua:
    -Copy the contents of basic_nitg_v491_feats.lua to somewhere in your Scripts folder. It doesn't matter which .lua file it's in, the game will
    load it all.
    (Unless the theme you're working with is really really old like this one, chances are some of the functions here already exist in the theme.
    It's highly recommended that you remove the duplicates.)
    
    -In BGAnimations folder, call the Gameplay function somewhere in ScreenGameplay, here it's at ScreenGameplay overlay:
    <ActorFrame Command="%GameplayInit" CurrentSongChangedMessageCommand="%GameplayInit" FirstUpdateCommand="%Gameplay"/>
    
    -In BGAnimations folder, call the ApplyNoteskin function somewhere in ScreenStage, here it's at ScreenStage in:
    <ActorFrame Command="%ApplyNoteskin"/>
    
    -In Graphics folder, copy the _Judgments and _HoldJudgments folders.
    
    -In metrics.ini, at [ScreenPlayerOptions], add the noteskin, judgment and hold judgment options in, for example:
    Line9=lua,NoteSkinOption()
    Line10=lua,JudgmentOption()
    Line11=lua,HoldJudgmentOption()
    and modify the LineNames accordingly.
    
    -In metrics.ini, add the resolution settings to your option screen of choice. Here it's at [ScreenScreenSettings].
    Make sure to modify the Y positions of the rows so that all LuaSetResolution rows have the same Y position.
